Monthly invoicing

On that day python -m app.cli monthly_invoicing (daily cron) builds one draft per opted-in open matter with unbilled time, expenses or a due milestone, then emails you a summary. With sending on, each draft goes straight to clients who have an email address; otherwise they wait under Invoices, Draft. Opt matters in on the matter form or all at once under Bulk invoicing. Each matter is billed at most once per month.
