Set OPENROUTER_API_KEY or ANTHROPIC_API_KEY in the environment, then turn AI on under Settings. AI_DAILY_CAP_CENTS (default 300) caps estimated spend per day. Spend is an estimate from token counts and is rounded up to whole cents.

Ask a question

Turns a plain question into filters over matters, contacts, invoices, time and tasks. When AI is unavailable the page falls back to a plain text search, so it always answers.

Where the other features live

  • Invoice narrative cleanup: the "AI: polish narratives" button on an invoice. Rewrites time line descriptions for the client; you see before and after and apply only on drafts.
  • Matter summary: the "AI summary" button on a matter's overview. About 150 words plus open items, with save-as-note.
  • Dates from a document: the "AI dates" button on the documents list. Finds deadlines and hearings in the file text and creates the ones you tick as tasks or events.
